# BADGE_SET.md

Badges are visible symbols on the ID Card.
Badges are not cosmetic collectables. They signal skill and consistency.

Badges display in two unlabeled columns:
- Certifications
- Streaks

## Certifications (Office Hours)
Certifications are Task-specific.
They are auto-awarded on completion of an Office Hours Task that defines a certification hook.

Examples:
- Tool certifications
- Illustrator skill certifications
- Safety certifications
- Process certifications

Rule:
- completing the required Office Hours Task publishes a certification badge automatically.

## Streaks
Streak badges replace the previous badge in the hierarchy.

### Log Streak
- Level 1: 5 published logs
- Level 2: 10 published logs
- Level 3: 20 published logs
- Level 4: 40 published logs
(you can tune thresholds later)

### Iteration Streak
Counts days where a student logs revision/iteration explicitly (tag or prompt-based).
- Level 1: 5 iteration logs
- Level 2: 10 iteration logs
- Level 3: 20 iteration logs
- Level 4: 40 iteration logs

Replacement rule:
If a student earns Level 2, Level 1 is removed from display (history can remain in data).
