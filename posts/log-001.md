# Exploring Data Visualization with Processing

Today I spent the afternoon diving into Processing, experimenting with how raw data can become expressive visual forms.

Started with a simple particle system that responds to audio input. The particles behave like schools of fish, moving together through an invisible field. When sound frequencies spike, they scatter. When it's quiet, they settle into calm patterns.

## Key experiments

* **Frequency mapping**: Each frequency band controls a different aspect of particle behavior
* **Color palettes**: Using HSB instead of RGB gives me more intuitive control over the mood
* **Performance**: Had to optimize the rendering loop—managing 10,000 particles requires careful shader work

The most interesting moment was when I played a piece of ambient music and watched the system respond organically. It felt like the data wasn't just being visualized—it was being *translated*.

## Next steps

* Export these explorations to p5.js for web implementation
* Experiment with 3D space instead of 2D
* Add interactive controls for live tweaking
