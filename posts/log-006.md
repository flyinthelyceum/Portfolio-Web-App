# Interactive Light Installation Simulation

## The Vision

Imagine a space where light responds to human presence not with motion sensors, but with a more poetic interpretation of movement and emotion.

## The Concept

This project explores how light can become a medium for conversation. The installation maps visitor movement through space and translates it into:

* **Warm/cool color shifts**: Faster movement = warmer light, slow presence = cooler tones
* **Intensity waves**: Creating a rhythm based on collective group movement
* **Spatial trails**: Traces of past movement linger briefly before fading

## Technical Implementation

Built as a web prototype using:
* **Canvas API**: For real-time particle and light rendering
* **Tracking simulation**: Stand-in for actual motion sensors in the physical installation
* **Generative patterns**: Creating organic, ever-changing light behaviors

## The Experience

Visitors become collaborators in creating light. There's immediate feedback but also a sense of unpredictability—the system responds but also has its own agency.

## Relevance to My Practice

This bridges my interests perfectly:
* **Technology**: Web graphics and real-time rendering
* **Art**: Creating emotional experiences through light
* **Interaction**: Making the digital and physical world converse

The most exciting possibility: deploying this as an actual installation where people's movements sculpt light in real space.
