# Motion Type: Animating Typography with Constraints

## The Challenge

Self-imposed rule: animate a single word using only position, scale, and rotation. No effects, no distortion, no opacity changes. Maximum 60 frames.

## Why Constraints Matter

When you remove options, you have to be more deliberate. Every choice has weight. The word "rhythm" becomes an exploration of timing and spacing, not visual effects.

## Process

1. Chose the word "drift"
2. Split it into individual letters
3. Experimented with patterns: staggered waves, spiraling rotations, pulsing scales
4. Found that combining all three dimensions created more interesting motion than any single parameter alone

## Key Discovery

The most compelling moment happens when all the chaos settles. After 45 frames of complex motion, bringing everything to rest in a single frame creates powerful punctuation.

## Next Steps

* Expand to full sentences
* Apply to interactive contexts
* Explore how constraint breeds creativity

This project reminds me why I love working with code and design together—the intersection of rules and creativity.
